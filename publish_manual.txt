1. Set 'Build Variants' to 'Release'
2. Press 'Build/Clean'
3. Press 'Build/Rebuild'
4. In terminal:
./gradlew publishRelease

The task publishes every release library module except `app` and
`biometric-custom-behavior`, then runs `common:postRelease` automatically.
